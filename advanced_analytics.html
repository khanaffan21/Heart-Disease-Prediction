Primary Diagnostic Predictors

ST Segment Slope

Flat or downsloping ST segments during stress testing are the #1 predictor of myocardial ischemia.

Exercise-Induced Angina

Chest pain triggered by exercise directly signals insufficient coronary blood flow.

ST Depression (Oldpeak)

ST depression >1.5 mm is a strong indicator of coronary artery stenosis.

Maximum Heart Rate

Chronotropic incompetence (inability to reach target max HR) correlates with cardiac impairment.

Serum Cholesterol

Elevated LDL cholesterol accelerates atheromatous plaque formation in coronary vessels.

Resting Blood Pressure

Hypertension (>130/80 mmHg) increases left ventricular workload and arterial wall stress.
