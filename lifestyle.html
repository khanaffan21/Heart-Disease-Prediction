🧘 Yoga & Exercise

Heart-Healthy Yoga Poses

Scientifically studied for cardiovascular benefits. Practice daily for 20–30 minutes.

🕊️
Savasana
Corpse Pose
❤️ Lowers Blood Pressure
Activates the parasympathetic nervous system, reducing cortisol and heart rate. The ultimate relaxation pose.
1
Lie flat on your back, arms at sides, palms up
2
Close eyes, breathe naturally and deeply
3
Hold for 10–15 minutes at end of practice
🦋
Baddha Konasana
Butterfly / Bound Angle Pose
🩸 Improves Circulation
Opens hips and groin, improving blood flow to the lower body. Reduces venous pressure and swollen ankles.
1
Sit with soles of feet pressed together
2
Gently flap knees like butterfly wings
3
Hold for 2–3 minutes with deep breathing
🌉
Setu Bandhasana
Bridge Pose
💪 Strengthens Heart
Strengthens back and core muscles supporting the heart. Opens the chest and improves cardiac output.
1
Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat on floor
2
Slowly lift hips toward the ceiling
3
Hold 30s–1 min, repeat 3 times
🏔️
Tadasana
Mountain Pose
🫁 Enhances Lung Capacity
Improves posture and opens the chest cavity, allowing deeper breathing and better oxygen exchange throughout the body.
1
Stand tall, feet together, arms at sides
2
Roll shoulders back, lift chest open
3
Breathe deeply for 2–3 minutes
🐱
Marjaryasana
Cat-Cow Pose
🧘 Calms Nervous System
Gentle spinal movement with breath activates the vagus nerve, slowing heart rate and reducing cortisol levels.
1
Start on hands and knees (tabletop)
2
Inhale: arch back (Cow); Exhale: round spine (Cat)
3
Repeat 10–15 times slowly
🦅
Viparita Karani
Legs-Up-The-Wall Pose
⬇️ Lowers BP Instantly
Reverses venous pooling in the legs. Demonstrated to lower blood pressure within 10 minutes of practice.
1
Sit sideways against a wall, then swing legs up
2
Lie flat with legs resting vertically on wall
3
Relax for 5–15 minutes
💡
Yoga Safety Note: If diagnosed with heart disease, consult your cardiologist before starting yoga. Avoid inversions and intense poses. Hot yoga is NOT recommended for cardiac patients.

Anulom Vilom (Alternate Nostril)
Balances the autonomic nervous system. Reduces BP and anxiety within 5 minutes.
How: Close right nostril → inhale through left → close left → exhale right → inhale right → exhale left. Repeat 10 rounds.
Benefit: 📉 Reduces BP by up to 15 points
🐝
Bhramari (Humming Bee)
Vibration activates the vagus nerve, instantly calming the cardiovascular system.
How: Cover ears, inhale deeply. On exhale, hum loudly. Feel vibration in your head. 5–7 rounds.
Benefit: ❤️ Reduces anxiety & irregular heartbeat
🌊
Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)
Used by Navy SEALs. Perfect for stress-induced heart rate spikes.
How: Inhale 4s → Hold 4s → Exhale 4s → Hold 4s. Repeat 4–6 rounds.
Best for: Acute stress, anxiety attacks
🥗 Nutrition

Heart-Healthy Diet Guide

The Mediterranean and DASH diets have the strongest scientific evidence for reducing cardiovascular risk.

✅ Eat More — Heart Protectors
🐟
Fatty Fish (Salmon, Mackerel, Sardines)
Omega-3 fatty acids reduce triglycerides & inflammation. 2 servings/week.
🥑
Avocados
Monounsaturated fats lower LDL and raise HDL. Rich in potassium for BP control.
🫐
Berries (Blueberries, Strawberries)
Anthocyanins reduce blood pressure and arterial stiffness. Eat daily.
🥦
Leafy Greens (Spinach, Kale)
High in nitrates — convert to nitric oxide, relaxing blood vessels and reducing BP.
🌰
Nuts (Walnuts, Almonds, Pistachios)
Healthy fats, magnesium, and fiber reduce cardiac risk by 30-35%.
🫒
Extra-Virgin Olive Oil
Polyphenols reduce inflammation. Use as your primary cooking fat.
🌾
Whole Grains (Oats, Quinoa, Brown Rice)
Beta-glucan fiber in oats lowers LDL by 5-10%. Aim for 3 servings/day.
🧄
Garlic
Allicin lowers blood pressure and reduces arterial plaque formation naturally.
❌ Limit or Avoid — Heart Hazards
🧂
Excess Sodium (Salt)
Max 2,300mg/day. Excess salt raises blood pressure — #1 risk factor for stroke.
🥩
Red & Processed Meats
High in saturated fats. Processed meats increase cardiac risk by 42%.
🧈
Trans Fats & Saturated Fats
Raise LDL and lower HDL simultaneously. Found in fried foods, margarine, pastries.
🥤
Sugary Drinks
Liquid sugar spikes glucose and triglycerides. Switch to water or green tea.
🍟
Deep-Fried Foods
Create oxidized fats that damage arterial walls and promote atherosclerosis.
🍕
Ultra-Processed Foods
High in additives, sodium, unhealthy fats. Each 10% increase raises cardiac risk by 12%.
🍷
Excess Alcohol
Raises blood pressure and triglycerides. Max: 1/day women, 2/day men.
🍬
Refined Carbs & Sugars
Promote weight gain, inflammation, and insulin resistance — all cardiac risk multipliers.
🌿
Mediterranean Diet Sample Day: Breakfast: Greek yogurt + berries + walnuts | Lunch: Grilled salmon + quinoa + spinach salad | Dinner: Lentil soup + whole grain bread + steamed veggies | Snack: Apple + almond butter | Target: <2g sodium, 25-35g fiber daily.

🧠 Mental Health

Mental Health & Heart Connection

Stress, anxiety, and depression directly increase cardiac risk. Mental health IS heart health.

😰
The Stress-Heart Connection
Chronic stress releases cortisol and adrenaline which raise blood pressure, promote inflammation and arterial damage, increase blood clotting risk, and lead to unhealthy coping behaviors. People with high chronic stress have 40-50% higher cardiac risk.
😔
Depression & Heart Disease
Depression is a significant independent risk factor. Depressed patients have 3x higher post-heart attack mortality. It increases inflammation markers (CRP, IL-6) and reduces motivation for healthy choices. If you feel persistently low, please seek help — treating depression improves cardiac outcomes.
🧘
Mindfulness & Meditation
Mindfulness meditation (10 min/day) reduces BP by 4-5 mmHg. Body scan reduces stress hormones within one session. Loving-kindness meditation reduces heart rate variability stress markers. Apps: Headspace, Calm, Insight Timer.
😴
Sleep & Cardiac Health
Less than 6 hours = 48% higher heart disease risk. Sleep apnea untreated = 2-4x higher cardiac risk. Target: 7-9 hours with consistent bed/wake times. Treat sleep apnea — CPAP therapy dramatically reduces cardiac events.
